The Filmanthropy Ledger · Social Effects NJ

We’d love to hear from crews, filmmakers and communities around the world. Share the good you’ve seen, what went to waste, or a possibility worth exploring.

A small observation can open a bigger conversation. You don’t need a polished story or a finished case study.

Tell us what happened, what you noticed or what you’d like to see happen. If you gave or received something, we’d love to hear what it made possible afterward.

For now, email Bret your story, observation or idea. Include your name, role and location, and any production or source links you can share. Please say whether you give permission to publish your contribution and how you’d like to be credited.

Bret reviews submissions before publication. Sending a contribution does not publish it automatically.

Email your story to Bret

Write to bret@socialeffectsnj.com. You can also suggest a case study or a news report for Happening Now.
