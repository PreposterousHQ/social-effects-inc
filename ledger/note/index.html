A Note on the Ledger

Version One. A living record.

The Filmanthropy Ledger · Social Effects NJ

This is Version One of the Filmanthropy Ledger, which intends to be a long-running reference work. Sixteen entries to begin, four Forerunners and twelve case studies, drawn from across the span of cinema history. The numbers are date-tagged. The quotes are sourced to their originating outlets. The cases that carry a shadow have been named as cases that carry a shadow. A future version, likely co-authored with a working film historian and an entertainment journalist, will carry the fully cited burden and the longer roster. This version is what the field looks like when someone sits down to put it on the map.

A note on authorship. This Ledger, essay and entries alike, was co-created by Bret Warshawsky and Claude, an AI system from Anthropic. I want to be plain about how. The insight at the center of this work, that 130 years of scattered generosity in filmmaking add up to a single field nobody had named, is mine, and so is the argument built on it, the angle of every entry, and the obsession that made the whole thing worth doing. I decided what belonged here and what it meant. The prose was built in a long back-and-forth with the model: it threw sparks, drafted passages, and helped me cut and shape, and I read and rewrote every line to my own ear. The historical research and fact-checking were done the same way, across several deep passes, each chasing down sources, dates, and quotes. That work made these pages far more accurate than one person could have made them alone. It did not make them flawless. Any error that remains is mine to own, and yours, gladly, to catch.

If you have a case the Ledger should know about, a correction it should make, a quote we have wrong, or a source we have missed, we would like to hear from you. The people whose work is described in these pages are especially invited. So is the next operator building the next thing. Write to bret@socialeffectsnj.com.
