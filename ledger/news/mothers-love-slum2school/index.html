Mother’s Love reports an education gift

Mother’s Love

NIGERIA · 2026-08-12 · RedHot Concepts · Omotola Jalade-Ekeinde · Slum2School Africa

Omotola Jalade-Ekeinde reports that the producers’ entire ₦22,276,558 share of the film’s Nigerian cinema run was transferred to Slum2School Africa on July 7, 2026, to support children’s education. That is the amount remaining for the producers after cinema, distribution and other deductions from a reported gross of more than ₦103 million. The distinction matters: the commitment concerns the producers’ receipts from that theatrical run. It is a reported fulfillment of a giving pledge, with the next chapter belonging to the children and communities the funds are meant to serve.

Reported transfer · filmmaker’s account · Transfer reported by the filmmaker · seek recipient confirmation and an update on how the funds were used
