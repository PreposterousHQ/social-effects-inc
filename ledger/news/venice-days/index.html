Venice Days pledges its profits

Venice Days

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA, USA · 2026-10-29 · Dir. Bradley Stryker · Producer Caitlin Stryker · Los Angeles filmmakers

An independent feature about a widowed father in recovery is pairing its story with a promise to its own community. The filmmakers pledge all profits to Los Angeles organizations working on recovery, housing insecurity and rebuilding after the fires, naming Safe Place for Youth, One Voice and St. Joseph Center. Its completed Seed&Spark campaign raised $51,205 for post-production. This is a project to follow through distribution: the giving model is declared, while the eventual profits and payments to the named organizations remain to be documented.
