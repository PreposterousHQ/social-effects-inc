Balan’s bus stop stays with the community

Balan: The Boy

WAYANAD, KERALA, INDIA · 2026-06-26 · Art director Ajayan Chalissery · Dir. Chidambaram

After filming wrapped, residents of Kunduvadi asked the crew to leave a bus stop built as a set. Nearly a year later, the shelter remains in daily use by residents and schoolchildren across the tribal hamlet’s seven settlements.
