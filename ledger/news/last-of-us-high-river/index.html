The Last of Us funds a youth legacy project

The Last of Us

HIGH RIVER, ALBERTA, CANADA · 2021-10-13 · HBO production · Town of High River

The production paid the town $100,000 for a youth legacy project: 80 percent for a bike park and pump track, and 20 percent for the Spitzee School playground. It separately paid $15,000 for three removed trees. Current status of the park still needs confirmation.
