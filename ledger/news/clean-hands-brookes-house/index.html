Clean Hands pledges a third of its profits to Brooke’s House

Clean Hands

DALLAS, TEXAS, USA · 2026-10-14 · Margate House Films · Dir. Jake Allyn

The production has committed one-third of the film’s profits to Brooke’s House, the Hagerstown, Maryland recovery center whose founding inspired the film. The pledge needs a follow-up after release: whether profits arrived, and how much reached Brooke’s House.
