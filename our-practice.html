Designed in from the start

The giving is part of the making.

Film has a long history of generosity. Filmanthropy by Design brings that spirit into production planning, so the giving can be part of how a film is made.

Start with what the filmmakers care about and what community partners want and can use. Planning early makes room for the right people, responsibilities and resources within the film’s budget and schedule.

The Filmanthropy Spectrum

These approaches can work together. Productions, crews and organizations including Film Biz Recycling, EcoSet and Earth Angel have helped show how giving through film can take many forms.

01 The Floor

Sustainable

Do less harm. Reduce, recycle, divert waste from the landfill. It lowers a production's footprint.

02 The Middle

Contributive

Share what a production can give: food, wardrobe, sets, equipment, time, skills, access or a share of proceeds. Giving may support a community organization, a cause or a foundation. These contributions matter whether planned early or made possible along the way.

03 The Summit

Regenerative

Create lasting community value: a place, a capability or a structure that continues after filming. For example, the skatepark built for Skater Girl in Rajasthan, or a training or mentorship program that outlives the shoot.

Regenerative filmmaking

What remains after the cameras leave?

Regenerative filmmaking aims to leave people and places with greater capacity to thrive after filming ends. That might be a shared asset, local skills, ongoing access or a way for people to participate in the value created.

Community partners help define what would be useful and who can carry it forward. The measure is what continues to serve people after the crew has gone.

The Human Gift: Beyond the Material

Some things can't be boxed up. But they can be coordinated.

Filmanthropy by Design is not only about what a production leaves behind in bins and boxes. It's about what it leaves behind in people. That presence is itself a gift, if someone builds the infrastructure to offer it. We want to be that someone.

Workshops & Masterclasses

Cast and crew lead sessions for local students, community organizations, and nonprofits: from acting to cinematography to production design.

Set Visits & School Days

Behind-the-scenes access for high schools, community colleges, and youth programs. Real sets. Real crew. Real possibility.

PA Apprenticeships

Local teenagers and young adults spend a day as production assistants, the first rung of a career ladder most never knew existed in their own town.

Dream Visits

Coordinated through community partners, productions can offer Make-A-Wish style moments: meaningful encounters between cast, crew, and individuals for whom it changes everything.

Mentorship Introductions

Connecting film professionals with aspiring local creatives: one conversation, one relationship, that outlasts the production.

Community Screenings & Q&As

Nonprofits, shelters, schools, and community centers host exclusive screenings with cast and crew, before the world sees it.

Imagine a cast member sitting with women and children of a domestic violence shelter for an afternoon. Not a photo op. A conversation. That is the infrastructure we want to build: not just the wardrobe arriving at the shelter, but the human being walking through the door.
