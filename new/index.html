One shared purpose

Four connected parts of one practice.

Our definition of Filmanthropy is the love of film and the love of man weaving together through the films we make and the ways we make them.

Film is a gift. Its making can be one, too. We document what is possible, bring the stories to people, teach the practice and help put it to work.

A word with a history

Introduced by Ted Leonsis in 2007, Filmanthropy has been carried forward by filmmakers, festivals and organizations. We honor the people who gave the word its meaning and momentum.

Rooted in New Jersey

Celebrating the momentum. Adding to it.

We are excited about New Jersey’s growing film industry and the work already happening across productions, crews, towns, schools and nonprofits. We want to help that momentum grow, with more opportunities for local work, learning, reuse and lasting community benefit.

Our work builds on what people already do, with practical ways to explore further possibilities together.
