We’re building Social Effects NJ as a foundation and an LLC, bringing together education, awareness, entertainment and practical services to expand what filmmaking makes possible for people and communities.

Filmanthropy in practice.

Our definition of Filmanthropy is the love of film and the love of humanity weaving together through the films we make and the ways we make them.

Film is a gift. Its making can be one, too.

Rooted in New Jersey

Celebrating the momentum. Adding to it.

We are excited about New Jersey’s growing film industry and the work already happening across productions, crews, towns, schools and nonprofits. We want to help that momentum grow, with more opportunities for local work, learning, reuse and lasting community benefit.

Social Effects NJ

One purpose. A foundation and a business.

Our proposed foundation and LLC are intended to work together across this shared practice. Research, public education, entertainment, creative work and practical services can support one another, with each activity’s responsibilities assigned as the structure develops.
