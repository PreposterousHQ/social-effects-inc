Who we are

About Social Effects NJ.

People, principles and a shared purpose: making filmmaking a gift to the communities it touches.

Values & Vision

The purpose behind the practice.

Our vision

A film industry where generosity and creativity are designed in. A New Jersey that proves it. And a wider culture learning to meet material abundance with a generosity of spirit equal to it.

Our mission

Bring research, education, entertainment and practical services together to design generosity and community social impact into filmmaking.

Our future profits are intended to support mental health reformation and the Moonshot: a feature film whose making leaves lasting community infrastructure.

How we work

Human first. Technology forward. Relationships, accountability and community priorities guide the work. AI and other tools support the people carrying it out.

Meet the people

Meet the people.

Bret Warshawsky

Founder & Chief Filmanthropist

Bret Warshawsky is a writer, speaker, futurist and the founder of Social Effects NJ. His work brings together filmmaking, evolutionary and spiritual philanthropy, whole-systems design and mental health reformation, with a practical question at its heart: what can we build that helps people and communities thrive?

Over more than a decade of international collaboration, he worked with the late futurist Barbara Marx Hubbard, helped develop the holonic software project Noomap, and co-created Synergy Hub 1.0 in Rotterdam, a living experiment in gift economy and collective intelligence. His approach draws on Buckminster Fuller’s comprehensivism: finding connections across disciplines and testing them in lived experience.

At Social Effects NJ, Bret is developing Filmanthropy by Design and Regenerative Filmmaking, supported by the research and conversations gathered in the Filmanthropy Ledger. His wider work includes The Syntony Times, the Mental Health Reformation and the developing docuseries Unbreakable Spirit, continuing a personal story he first shared in Lisa Nichols’s 2011 book of the same name.

Andrew Alan

Creative and Strategic Narrative Advisor

Andrew Alan is a network weaver, storytelling strategist, writer, director, producer, and editor working across moving images, sound, words, and ideas. Born and raised in Berkeley, California and based in Europe, Andrew brings a transatlantic creative perspective shaped by a decade of concepting and visioneering across documentary, narrative, and strategic communications work. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Film Production, Screenwriting, and Directing from New York University's Tisch School of the Arts. For Social Effects NJ, Andrew brings the rare combination of someone who understands how stories get made and how they land in communities, the connective tissue between a production's creative intent and the real-world relationships that make Filmanthropy possible to execute.
