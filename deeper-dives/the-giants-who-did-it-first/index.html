A Century of Giving Through Film

The Giants Who Did It First

By Bret Warshawsky · Filmanthropy by Design and Regenerative Filmmaking

In brief

Giving through film has a history that long predates today’s language of social impact. This essay traces people and precedents from that history, showing how filmmakers have extended their work’s gifts beyond the screen and offering starting points for what productions could make possible next.
