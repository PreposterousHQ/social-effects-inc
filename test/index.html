Filmanthropy by Design

Begin before the
cameras roll.

Filmanthropy is the practice of making philanthropy a featured, designed-in outcome of the filmmaking process itself, not a side effect, not a press release, not an afterthought.

We’re developing engagements for independent filmmakers, producers and studios, shaped to the scope and resources of each production.

Discuss a production ↗
  1. 01
    Discovery

    Find the opportunities across materials, talent, local economies, community needs and the production’s ambitions.

  2. 02
    Design

    Build a practical plan for giving, participation, skills, assets and legacy from pre-production onward.

  3. 03
    Liaison

    Develop the relationships between a production, its host community and the people who can put resources to use.

  4. 04
    Story

    Document what happens, who benefits and what is learned, so the production’s social effects remain visible.

Film Ready Filmanthropy

Your town is ready.
What comes next?

A community can be more than a location. We envision working with municipalities, businesses, schools and productions to design benefits into the filming process.

This is a complementary community-benefit offering, separate from New Jersey’s official Film Ready certification.

Explore your town’s possibilities ↗
  1. 01
    Map the local possibilities

    Understand community priorities, local organizations, businesses, spaces and skills.

  2. 02
    Connect people and production

    Explore local purchasing, workforce pathways, practitioner learning and community participation.

  3. 03
    Design a lasting contribution

    Identify useful materials, shared assets and capabilities that could remain after wrap.

  4. 04
    Tell the whole story

    Track outcomes with the community and carry what is learned into future productions.

Film Biz Recycling, and beyond

Surplus is
unrealized value.

We’re seriously exploring a New Jersey recovery and reuse operation informed by Film Biz Recycling and other experienced practitioners.

A possible Savannah affiliation or franchise, operator participation and operating arrangements remain to be established. The ambition extends from physical recovery into a fuller regenerative production service.

Help build the operation ↗
  1. 01
    Materials & props

    Explore recovery, reuse, rental and redistribution of production resources.

  2. 02
    Recovery & logistics

    Develop practical collection, storage and distribution capacity with experienced operators.

  3. 03
    Recipient relationships

    Connect resources to actual community needs, with attention to usefulness and recipient outcomes.

  4. 04
    Production & community outcomes

    Document both resources kept in circulation and the value they create for people.
